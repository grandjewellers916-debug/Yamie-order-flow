Settings

Pricing rules (making conventions)▾

Making charges, wastage %, labour, fair value (FVA) and diamond price by karat, weight, product type, making type, supplier, NLC and more. The first rule that matches an item is used; it can override the database or only fill empty values.

Special promotions▾

Offers applied to every quote, for all users, while they run. The best offer that applies is taken off before GST. Users see the running offers when they open the app.

ReportsQuotes · sales▾
Export stock databaseExcel · PDF▾

Every item in the stock list with its product photo. Photos are fetched from the shared database, so a large stock list can take a minute.

Remove sold tags▾

Scan or type the barcodes of sold items, then delete them from the stock list.

Sold labels register

Labels of closed sales. They cannot be priced, quoted or loaded from a stock sheet again. Restore one only when a sale is cancelled or the piece is returned (admin).

Theme · style and colours▾

Applies to every phone and tablet in the shop.

Quote approval▾

When on, staff save a quote and send it to a manager or admin, who sets the discount and approves it (it then gets its QT number). Staff can share it only after approval.

Footer · tabs for staff▾

Tap a tab to show or hide it for that role. Home is always shown; admins see every tab.

Product details · items shown▾

Tick the items to show on the Product Details card. Items with no value are skipped anyway.

This computer · log-out & alerts▾

For this computer or phone only. Staff stay logged in until they log out; on a shared shop computer, log out after it has not been used for a while.

Text size▾

Make everything smaller if the rates or prices don't fit the screen.

Product image · show / hide▾

Show the product photo (or item icon) on the price card. Photos are added from the Search tab.

Rates shown▾

Purities shown on the rate band, the day's rates screen and the purity buttons. Shown gold rates are required each day.

Charges · GST · HUID▾
Making helpers▾

CZ jewellery

MBD = customer price (before discount), MAD = staff lowest (after discount). Values: 35%, 1700 (per piece), 1700 PG (per gram). Stone = ₹ per unit of SWT.

Diamond jewellery

Item can be Nosepin, Other Jewellery, or a supplier / item name (e.g. MANOJ) to match. Diamond = ₹ per carat.

Making conventions · label check▾

The usual making and fair value (FVA) for each item type, shown to all staff under Making guide on Home and checked against the label after a scan. Values: 28%, 1500 PG (per gram), 800 (per piece). Leave purity on Any to cover every purity.

Suppliers▾

Every supplier entered: the ones in this list and the ones on stock items (shown faded until added). Used as the drop-down list in New item, Edit database, Bulk update, pricing rules and Search. Renaming one offers to change it on its stock items too.

Item codes▾

Barcode prefix and item name, one per line, e.g. BGL = Bangle. Used to name items from their barcode.

Shared database · shop key▾

Connect every phone with the same shop key to share stock, day's rates, the making helper and users. Phones sync each morning (first open from 6 AM) and after a stock change; tap Sync now any time.

Not connected. This phone keeps its own data.

Users▾

Everyone logs in with their own name and PIN. What they can do depends on their role.
